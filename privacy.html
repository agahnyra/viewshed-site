Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026 · Developer: Agah Emre Çelebi · Contact: customerservice@nyrainteractive.com

Summary

Viewshed is a puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. It does not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, location or photos. The data it handles is listed below, with why and who receives it.

1. Data stored only on your device

  • Game progress, settings, passport stamps, streaks and hint tokens.
  • A local gameplay log (capped at 2 MB) used only to tune puzzle difficulty if you choose to send it to us. It never leaves the device by itself.
  • A small signed record of your daily energy and ad pacing, kept in the iOS Keychain so it survives reinstalling the app on the same device. It is not synced to other devices.

2. Anonymous player identifier (Unity Gaming Services)

When the app is online it signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies). This creates a random player ID that is not linked to your name or Apple ID. It is used to keep your daily energy count on the server so it cannot be tampered with, to rank your Daily Dispatch result against other players' results (score and time only, no names), and to verify a Viewshed Plus purchase for that player ID.

Data sent: player ID, energy state, daily results, purchase entitlement status. Provider policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

3. Purchases (Apple, RevenueCat)

Viewshed Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card. RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) processes the App Store receipt to grant the Plus entitlement and links it to the anonymous player ID above. Data: receipt, product, purchase and expiry dates, player ID. Provider policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

Free players see interstitial ads at the end of a run and may choose to watch a rewarded ad for extra energy. Ads are served by Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it mediates (currently Unity Ads). These networks receive device information (model, OS version, language, IP address, app version) and, only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt, the advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. If you decline, ads still work but are not personalised. You can change the choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Plus subscribers see no ads. Provider policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Notifications

Reminders (next daily, streak at risk, weekly goals) are scheduled locally on the device. No push server is involved. Turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in iOS Settings.

6. Sharing

The Share button opens the iOS share sheet with a text and an image you generated. Nothing is sent anywhere until you pick an app.

7. Children

Viewshed is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no account data, most requests can be met by deleting the app, which removes all local data except the Keychain record (removed when the device is erased). To have the server-side record for your anonymous player ID deleted, e-mail customerservice@nyrainteractive.com from the device you play on. EU/UK residents have the rights under the GDPR and California residents the rights under the CCPA; we do not sell personal data.

9. Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new effective date.

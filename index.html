Read the land. Find the spot.

A map-reading puzzle for iPhone.

Every round shows you a ground-level photo and a hand-drawn map of the same invented landscape. Work out where the photo was taken, and on harder rounds which way the camera was facing. Four kinds of map, a free Daily Dispatch every day, a passport to fill.

Every world, photo and map is generated from the same imaginary terrain. No real places, no satellite imagery.

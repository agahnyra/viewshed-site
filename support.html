Support

Write to customerservice@nyrainteractive.com. We usually answer within two working days.

Common questions

I bought Plus but the app still shows adsOpen Settings (gear on Home) → Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple ID that made the purchase.
How do I cancel the subscription?iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Viewshed Plus → Cancel. Or Settings in the app → Manage subscription.
Out of energy?Free players get 3 Journey or Practice runs a day, plus one more per rewarded ad (up to 2 a day). The first Daily Dispatch of the day is always free; playing it again costs a run. Energy resets at midnight local time. The first two Journey expeditions are free; the rest, the daily archive and live rooms come with Viewshed Plus, which has no energy limit.
The map is hard to readTap the layers button in the bottom-left corner of the map to toggle layers, or turn on High-contrast grid and Colour-blind friendly colours in Settings.
Reminders don't arriveCheck iOS Settings → Notifications → Viewshed, and Settings → Reminders inside the app.
I lost my progress / I have a new phoneYour progress (expeditions, stamps, streak, daily results) is backed up to our server. If you delete and reinstall the app on the same iPhone, it comes back. On a new phone it comes back if you sign in to the same Game Center account; without Game Center it can't be moved. Settings are not copied. Viewshed Plus can always be restored with Restore purchases.
My friends don't show upSign in to Game Center in the iPhone's Settings app and allow Viewshed to see your friends (tap the friends card on Home, then Friends → Allow friends). Friends appear once they play Viewshed too. Accounts restricted by Screen Time can't use friends or live rooms.
Live roomsLive rooms come with Viewshed Plus, for whoever opens a room and for everyone who joins. On Home, tap Open a room and share its code; friends use Join with a code. Up to 8 players answer the same photos at the same time. Rooms need an internet connection.

Reporting a bug

Send the iPhone model, iOS version, the app version (Settings → About, at the bottom; Email us there fills it in for you) and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot helps.

Your data

In the game, Settings → Game Center → "Delete my data" deletes everything our server keeps about you and this device's progress. For anything else (a copy of your data, a deletion request to our partners) write to us. See the Privacy Policy.

Support

Write to customerservice@nyrainteractive.com. We usually answer within two working days.

Common questions

I bought Plus but the app still shows adsOpen Settings (gear on Home) → Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple ID that made the purchase.
How do I cancel the subscription?iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Viewshed Plus → Cancel. Or Settings in the app → Manage subscription.
Out of energy?Free players get 3 Journey or Practice runs a day, plus one more per rewarded ad. The Daily Dispatch is always free. Energy resets at midnight local time.
The map is hard to readOpen the Layers sheet (top of the map) to toggle layers, or turn on High-contrast grid and Colour-blind friendly colours in Settings.
Reminders don't arriveCheck iOS Settings → Notifications → Viewshed, and Settings → Reminders inside the app.
I lost my progressProgress is stored on the device. Deleting the app deletes it; there is no account to restore from yet. Purchases can always be restored.

Reporting a bug

Send the iPhone model, iOS version, the app version (Settings → About) and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot helps.

Data requests

To delete the server-side record for your anonymous player ID, e-mail us from the device you play on. See the Privacy Policy.
